# Research Philosophy

Use this reference for mathematical or physical concept development, derivation,
construction, compression, and reconstruction. Begin in the inquiry's practice,
not in a presumed universal vocabulary. Formalization, typed structure, and an
obstruction argument are selected methods; descriptive inquiry, measurement,
replication, literature synthesis, and accepted exposition need not invent them.

## Research preferences

Favor expressions in which fewer independent data and explicit relations generate
useful consequences, making repeated expansions or repairs unnecessary. These are
preferences for selecting questions, methods, and further investment, not gates
every result or turn must pass. Let the user's question and priorities govern;
baseline work, descriptive results, and promising incomplete ideas retain value.

When effective use matters, investigate how the needed evaluation, transformation,
normalization, or decision can actually be obtained; a contract alone supplies no
procedure, and a paper-and-pencil procedure need not become code.

For semantic reduction, select a scientifically consequential family of questions
or operations and investigate which independent information it needs. Construct
an expression that preserves those uses while forgetting irrelevant distinctions;
test it against cases that the proposed expression merges. A quotient label or
short formula is insufficient when the original work is hidden in its evaluation,
equivalence test, or recovery. The family need not recover the entire theory, but
must not shrink merely to make the proposed reduction succeed.

Prefer invariant, quotient, symmetry-adapted, normal-form, spectral, or
variational reasoning when it shortens or strengthens the complete route. Let
relations and operations carry the derivation, with needed component expressions
recoverable. Use components when they are the observable representation, no
structural reduction is known, or an independent check needs them; return the
conclusion to its invariant meaning. Fewer indices alone do not establish a gain.

## Develop concepts through consequential use

Start from domain constructions, theorem hypotheses, observations and permitted
operations. Reconstruct material into the relation producing the phenomenon,
including contrary cases and conditions under which it changes. Where one process
supports and undermines a capability, derive how these tendencies arise together
and why the present form reproduces their opposition. Expand a failed equality,
unresolved composition or recurring repair; compare cases the attempt merges but
the required operation separates. A productive relation already visible in
successful work can also start a construction; failure is not a prerequisite.

Let the user's research preferences shape what to construct from that mechanism.
Seek a relation that preserves successful uses while generating repeated repairs
from shared data, making calculations or distinctions unnecessary, or exposing
genuinely independent data. A research bet connects this candidate, a consequence
still to be derived, and the scientific work it could replace or enable. Its
plausibility comes from the domain structure; its value comes from that scientific
use. A factorization,
symmetry or transferred method earns attention through what it lets one derive.

To reconstruct a concept, distinguish content required by the question from
conditions imposed by its current expression. Make the specific negation carry
construction content: which determination fails, which relation survives, and
what operation can reorganize them? Construct the resulting object and operations;
show how earlier valid content and successful uses survive as cases, approximations
or components, what is rejected, and which consequential use becomes possible.
An altered object distinction or judgment criterion needs a bridge to the
originating question.
This transformation must be carried by the derivation or construction, not merely
by naming a new concept.

Attempt the consequence using the domain's operations: solve an unsettled
cancellation or compatibility equation where possible, then carry the candidate
into a further composition, order or admissible input. Identify which relations
are forced and which choices remain free. Keep unproved premises conjectural;
the desired equality cannot supply its own proof. New assumptions remain research
debt until derived or admitted, and restoring all discarded inputs changes the
bet. These are construction moves, not a fixed sequence or proposal template.

Do not treat `ontology`, `primitive`, `kernel`, `closure`, or similar terms as
neutral prerequisites. A concept is provisionally stabilized with its relevant
neighbors through its examples and counterexamples, contrasts, permitted moves,
consequences, and correction after breakdown. Inspect only relations that can
affect the inquiry; these are possible observations, not a definition of meaning.

A definition is a proposal or local stipulation before its role is established.
Within a formal theory, syntax and rules may fix that role exactly for the stated
purpose. In an active inquiry, treat the concept as fixed only as far as relevant
new cases preserve the needed moves and contrary cases lead to inspectable local
revision. Change affected neighbors together; neither assume one word is fixed
independently nor rebuild the whole language.

When a developing concept admits competing accounts or opposing effects that
change the result, construct a consequential opposition rather than only a
convenient positive example: an admissible rival account may produce the
same observations yet require a different move, or one relation may support and
undermine the target under different conditions. Derive the condition or
transition that separates those cases. Seek alternative mathematical structures
across disciplines by the operation they preserve, not by verbal resemblance;
state what maps, what fails to map, and the distinct consequence produced. A
cross-domain analogy is a proposal, not evidence for the target domain. Do not
force this search on a settled derivation or a task whose result does not depend
on the concept's reconstruction.

## Expose formal construction when selected

When a claim depends on formal derivation, state the objects and domain with the
types needed to make the operation legal. Show the map, composition, quotient,
substitution, variation, limit, algorithm, or other operation; bring both sides
to a common target; and expose the evaluation, witness, or certificate together
with assumptions and the failure boundary.

An equation must arise from its operation rather than appear first and acquire an
interpretation afterward. A definition does not establish existence, uniqueness,
equivalence, invariance, necessity, or usefulness. A theorem contract may bridge
a step only when its exact hypotheses, output, role, and boundary match.

When moving from a practice concept to mathematics, expose which distinctions,
relations, compositions, or transformations the required operation must retain;
select the smallest representation that does so before choosing a numerical
proxy, estimator, or proof technique. If the available observation map identifies
distinct admissible states as one, record the consequent limit and the minimal
additional structure or assumption rather than refining the same representation.

When the inquiry asks why an object or operation is necessary, expose the failure
that forces additional structure:

```text
required capability
  -> cheapest typed candidate
  -> failed equality, ambiguity, residual, or obstruction
  -> additional structure forced by that failure
  -> constructed object and operations
  -> witness, consequence, and refusal boundary
```

When the starting foundation cannot reasonably be derived locally, state an exact
theorem contract. Without a necessity argument, a structure may still be an
ansatz, cited input, useful definition, or exploratory proposal; label that role
instead of calling it forced.

A reusable construction retains an operation for new admissible inputs. Its input
must not encode the expected answer. Distinguish regression on known input,
transfer to a structurally changed admissible input, and downstream use against
the complete baseline route. Regression alone verifies preservation; transfer
alone does not establish computational gain.

## Bound productivity and improvement claims

Do not call a field, theory, or concept simply computable or noncomputable. Name
the represented task and claim:

- conceptual productivity introduces a distinction, canonical representation,
  question, or relation that changes subsequent inquiry;
- deductive productivity derives consequences under stated rules; and
- effective productivity supplies an algorithm, normalization, decision
  procedure, or executable construction for a declared encoding and domain.

Evidence for one does not establish the others. A new concept may be provisionally
useful because an obstruction forces it, because its relations produce a
consequence or compression not encoded in the motivating case, or because it
selects a discriminating next inquiry. None requires claiming universal reach.

Claim that an incumbent cannot produce a result only relative to stated language,
operations, domain, target, and resources, supported by a matched invariant,
undefinability result, lower bound, indistinguishability argument, or no-go
theorem. A counterexample may defeat a matching universal claim but does not by
itself prove inability. Otherwise report the observed comparative limitation, not
absolute inability.

Distinguish improvement claims:

```text
reformulation   same relevant information in another representation
compression     less information or work for a named result
reconstruction  changed governing distinctions or dynamics with controlled recovery
```

An invertible change may improve sparsity, conditioning, locality, or recursion
without being a new theory. A quotient is useful only relative to distinctions a
named result cannot see. Freeze the required result and quality before claiming a
cost gain; a faster route that loses a required distinction is not cheaper for
that result. Use [evidence adjudication](evidence-adjudication.md) for a contested
complete-route comparison.

For a theory-reconstruction claim, make that recovery bridge, any approximation
error, and the new discriminating consequence explicit. Failed approximations,
proliferating repairs, singular behavior, or collective structure can motivate
reconstruction; none proves it. Separate kinematics from dynamics, classification
from realization, convention from invariant, symmetry from selected dynamics, and
exact statements from approximations.

Finite derivation stays adjacent to the argument. Select
[computation](computation.md) only when execution must produce a required result or
carry inferential weight; conceptual or deductive productivity does not imply
code.
