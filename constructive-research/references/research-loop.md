# Research Loop

Use this reference while an inquiry frontier can still change. It owns action
selection, frontier transitions, retention, and stopping. For a frozen or
contested claim, add [evidence adjudication](evidence-adjudication.md); do not load
that protocol for a self-contained derivation or accepted exposition.

## Keep one visible transition

```text
read the frontier
  -> select one discriminating action
  -> derive, inspect an admitted source/observation, or admit execution
  -> admit a bounded result, uninformative result, or classified failure
  -> decide the smallest frontier change or no change
  -> retain, compact, or drop newly produced material
  -> choose the next obligation or stop
```

The frontier needs only the active question or frozen claim, weakest consequential
obligation, live alternatives, horizon, and stop/re-entry condition. Formal
contract versions, evidence closures, kernels, and symbolic disposition regions
are optional aids for contested or persistent campaigns; they are not mandatory
state for every inquiry.

An action qualifies when its possible outcomes distinguish live alternatives,
construct a missing object or operation, establish a boundary, or change a named
consumer. If every outcome has the same consequence, it is not a discriminating
probe. Choose the least effectful adequate mode. A new measurement or acquisition
requires its own authority and effect boundary; execution must pass
[computation admission](computation.md).

## Discover a research spine

Begin with the requested capability or phenomenon, current account and
presumptions, available material, live alternatives, weakest missing bridge, and
horizon. Do not invent a claim or obstruction merely to populate a workflow.

1. Name the mismatch between the requested result and the current account.
2. State alternatives or a relation whose success and failure have different
   consequences.
3. Select the smallest operation that can expose the distinction.
4. Advance only when the result eliminates or merges an alternative, exposes a
   presumption, constructs an object/operation, or establishes a boundary.
5. Form a candidate spine only from supported objects, relations, operations,
   outputs, falsifiers, and remaining bridges.

If the mismatch was ill-typed, revise the inquiry explicitly. Descriptive work may
instead end in a bounded account of an observed phenomenon; it need not manufacture
a generative construction.

## Admit a result before changing the frontier

Material becomes usable evidence only when its target, domain, operation, source
identity, provenance, witness or observed value, uncertainty, boundary, and
permitted consequence are sufficient for the claim. Keep unavailable, failed,
partial, stale, contradictory, and uninformative results distinct.

An admitted result cannot revise its own target. For a stable uncontested inquiry,
state the bounded consequence directly. When a claim is frozen, evidence conflicts,
reproduction matters, or literature coverage supports novelty/absence, use
[evidence adjudication](evidence-adjudication.md) before changing the claim.

For extracted documents, consume the located projection and artifact result from
`document-artifacts`; preserve source revision, locators, reading-order or
transcription uncertainty, and incomplete coverage. Extraction success alone does
not validate the research claim.

## Retain only decision-bearing material

- **Promote** material that changes a future decision, supplies a named consumer,
  or preserves a consequential result or refusal.
- **Compact** a negative, failed, or boundary result to the smallest record that
  prevents repeated work or unsafe reuse.
- **Drop** newly produced scratch or reproducible output with no future decision,
  consumer, recovery, or refusal value.

Retention changes storage, not inferential force, and never authorizes deletion of
pre-existing user material. Use [research state](research-state.md) only when a
durable dependency, canonical owner binding, or multiple projection consumes it.

## Stop or re-enter

Stop and synthesize when the requested result is supported within scope, a bridge
is supported or rejected, further detail cannot change a consumer, the declared
horizon or resource plateau is met, or the next discriminating action is unavailable
or unauthorized. Report the supported spine, weakest open bridge, and parked
alternatives with exact re-entry conditions.

Revise a candidate only when admitted inputs/outputs, preserved semantics,
generation, refusal, or a core boundary changes. An unwanted result, a newly fluent
alternative, or elapsed time is not enough. Preserve the changed premise, affected
consequences, unchanged invariants, and evidence that caused the revision.
