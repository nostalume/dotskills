# Evidence Adjudication

Use this reference for a frozen or contested claim, heterogeneous evidence,
failed reproduction, or a literature conclusion whose coverage matters. It owns
claim-local evidence closure and disposition; the [research loop](research-loop.md)
owns the surrounding frontier transition and retention.

## Freeze the claim before decisive evidence

Freeze only the fields that can change the verdict: claim and dimensions, domain
and presumptions, admitted input/output and preserved semantics, falsifiers and
promotion conditions, evidence modalities or benches, observable/error/cost target,
synthesis trigger, horizon, and re-entry conditions. A structured contract or
version label is useful when evidence is contested or replayed, but is not a
mandatory file or schema.

Examples do not add candidate rules case by case. A seed can expose a construction
but cannot establish independent transfer. Choose only bench families needed by
the claim: regression, structurally different transfer, adversarial/refusal, and
downstream use or complete-route cost.

## Give each evidence kind its exact right

| Kind | May establish | Cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deductive witness | exact support under matched hypotheses | wider applicability |
| counterexample | rejection or restriction in its matched domain | replacement theory |
| certified computation | result under its arithmetic/error policy | a general theorem |
| empirical observation | support under its measurement model | universality |
| theorem contract | implication after exact hypothesis matching | internal generation |
| regression | preservation of known behavior | transfer or novelty |
| transfer bench | unchanged use across tested variation | unlimited generality |
| cost/use bench | leverage for the same output and resource model | semantic correctness |
| literature protocol | located precedent or bounded coverage | absolute absence or novelty |

Evidence records an observation; it cannot mutate the claim, admission policy, or
methodology. Preserve target and dimension, domain match or explicit delta,
operation, source/implementation revision, provenance and independence, witness or
data, error/uncertainty, tested alternatives, and permitted consequence. Exclude
invalid or mismatched material explicitly when its reason matters for replay.

## Close evidence over domain and time

At a declared cutoff, include supporting, contrary, and conflicting evidence that
targets the claim, intersects its domain and dimension, passes the frozen admission
rules, and is not withdrawn, invalid, or superseded. Partition each claim dimension
into supported, refuted, unresolved, or live-conflict regions rather than assigning
one scalar confidence. Symbols such as `C_v`, `K`, `A_t`, or `D+`/`D-`/`D?`/`Dx`
may compress a complex durable campaign; the semantic distinctions, not the
notation, are mandatory.

For each material region, name the warrant, exact coverage, independence,
reproducibility, conflict state, untested alternatives, and bounded consequence.
Headline labels are summaries, never the only record.

## Synthesize one disposition

Synthesize when a decisive condition fires, planned discriminators complete or
become unavailable, same-domain conflict appears, a consumer needs a bounded
verdict, the horizon is met, or the user requests an interim view. Freeze admitted
and excluded evidence, cutoff, trigger, and governing rules. Identical structured
inputs must yield the same structured disposition even if prose differs.

Apply these precedence rules:

- Invalid or mismatched evidence has no claim effect.
- An exact in-domain counterexample defeats the matching universal region.
- Proof/counterexample conflict remains live until hypothesis or operation mismatch
  is resolved; positive examples cannot outvote it.
- Positive evidence supports only its domain and dimension.
- Regression cannot promote transfer; transfer cannot establish cost gain; cost
  failure does not refute correctness.
- A theorem-contract mismatch leaves the bridge open rather than making it false.
- Evidence alone never propagates or revises the claim.

Return support, restriction, rejection, split, conflict, or an honest
underdetermined disposition by domain and dimension. Record the admitted/excluded
evidence, bounded consequence, affected consumers, and re-entry condition. Only
then may the loop update the frontier.

## Handle failed reproduction and literature

A failed reproduction is new evidence, not automatic refutation. First classify
environment or data drift, protocol/input/unit mismatch, implementation defect,
numerical or stochastic instability, hidden assumption, statistical
non-replication, semantic target mismatch, original-result failure, or unresolved
cause. Restrict only the region reached by that cause; unresolved cases remain
conflicted or open and cannot propagate success.

Before literature search, declare the question and synonyms, source classes,
date/language/discipline bounds, inclusion/exclusion rules, citation paths,
competing terminology, and inaccessible classes. Stop at purpose-relative
saturation: authoritative and citation paths are covered and successive searches
add no source that changes the claim, precedent, or boundary map. The strongest
negative result is only that no prior work was found within the declared protocol
and coverage.

Changing admission or precedence rules is a methodology revision. Preserve the
old and new rules, smallest semantic delta, affected dispositions, and replay
obligation; do not revise policy merely to obtain a preferred result.
