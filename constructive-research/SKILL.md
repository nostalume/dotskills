# Constructive Research

Own mathematical or physical research meaning and its evidential boundary. The
user and canonical sources own intent and supplied material; observations own only
what their method supports. Artifact formats, product code, and ordinary business
analysis remain with their respective skills.

## Shared responsibility

```text
question, admitted material and authority
  -> phenomenon, working construction, claim, or requested result
  -> unresolved operation and its owner
  -> least effectful adequate derivation, construction, source, observation or execution
  -> evidence with exact inferential right and boundary
  -> bounded disposition, open obligation or honest unavailability
  -> durable state or reader/artifact projection only for a named consumer
```

Develop research meaning by reconstructing material into a governing relation,
constructing its consequences, and letting those consequences revise the inquiry.
For concept or theory reconstruction, use research philosophy; let the resulting
construction govern the next research move.

Preserve the distinctions, relations, operations, examples, counterexamples,
domains, uncertainty, conflicts, and refusal conditions that matter to the
selected research practice. Use typed objects and invariants when the formal
operation requires them; do not impose that vocabulary on descriptive or
conceptual work. A definition, citation, observation, proof, or successful run
establishes only its admitted kind of support. Do not claim generality, novelty,
reconstruction, or computational leverage beyond the tested domain and
comparison.

Keep the originating question or direction distinct from the result currently
supported. A narrower theorem, easier case, or changed question does not discharge
the original obligation. Make its contribution and remaining gap explicit; carry
that gap through continuation, synthesis, and stopping.

## Select the unresolved operation

Read only the owners needed by the current operation:

- **Develop, derive, or construct:** practice-grounded concept development,
  finite derivation, theorem contracts, conditional obstruction-driven
  construction, compression, or theory reconstruction:
  [research philosophy](references/research-philosophy.md).
- **Discover:** develop and pursue a provisional research construction within an
  open direction, revise it through derivation and failure, allocate effort, or
  stop: [research loop](references/research-loop.md).
- **Adjudicate:** freeze a claim, admit heterogeneous evidence, resolve conflict or
  failed reproduction, bound literature coverage, and emit a disposition:
  [evidence adjudication](references/evidence-adjudication.md), together with the
  research loop only when the frontier can change.
- **Persist:** bind durable dependencies, canonical material owners, multiple
  projections, or retained artifacts: [research state](references/research-state.md).
- **Execute:** admit and perform substantial symbolic, numerical, combinatorial,
  simulation, data, or plotting work: [computation](references/computation.md).
- **Explain:** project already supported content into a paper, note, or figure:
  [research writing](references/research-writing.md). Add the producing owner only
  when a derivation, evidence status, or durable binding remains unresolved.

Load only the current operation's guidance: bounded derivation needs no campaign
schema, one-output exposition no state index, and a requested plot no new
scientific claim. Missing sources, tools or consumers affect only their route.
Preserve supported results; when only physical representation remains, hand the
semantic projection to `document-artifacts`.

## Admit actions and effects

Use the least effectful adequate operation. A derivation, matched theorem contract,
admitted source, or already available observation may close an obligation without
execution. Select computation only after its admission gate.

Treat supplied or previously acquired observations as candidate material whose
identity, method, domain, uncertainty, and permitted consequence still require
admission. Evidence-admission authority does not authorize a new measurement,
instrument or human interaction, paid acquisition, credential use, remote data
disclosure, or other external effect. Resolve that operation's owner, authority,
bounds, consent/security obligations, and receipt before acting; otherwise return
the supported partial result and exact unavailable observation.

## Advance and stop

1. For an open direction, recover the user's question and constraints, then
   develop and pursue a research bet before repairing an inherited route; use the
   research loop. For a fixed task, enter at its unresolved operation. Do not
   replay settled work.
2. Apply its primary owner's transition and import constraints by reference.
3. Admit new material once. Only an adjudicated disposition changes a frozen
   claim; only a named durable consumer admits persistent state or an artifact.
4. Stop on scoped completion, a declared horizon, no worthwhile continuation, or
   unavailable or unauthorized remaining routes. Report the supported result
   against the originating target, weakest open obligation, and re-entry
   condition.

Keep research material with its canonical worktable owner. Use confirmed-ignored
agent space only for transient cursors, audits, or plans; ignore status is not
confidentiality or publication control. Keep those working records distinct from
reader-facing outputs, and do not reorganize or delete user material merely to fit
this model.
