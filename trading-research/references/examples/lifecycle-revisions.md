# Confirmation, Revision, and Recovery Examples

Constructed cases use [validation](../knowledge/inference-validation.md) and
[lifecycle](../knowledge/lifecycle-computation.md).

## A revised source invalidates the affected descendants

Version A joins a March fundamental value dated to 31 March, despite availability
10 April. Version B corrects availability. The research relation may survive, but
features/predictions before 10 April and their fit/confirmation are affected.
Recompute those descendants. A corrected integer cache format that preserves
identical values/times instead needs implementation equivalence checks, not a new
market hypothesis. This distinction keeps repair tied to what actually changed.

## A high IC can fail its first execution consumer

Assume two shortlisted factors use the same confirmed date set. One offers faster
forecast improvement but much larger turnover; the other is slower. Compare each
increment within the same gate/holding/cost policy at a frozen size ladder. Retain
the first factor as rejected or size-restricted if costs consume its increment,
with the observed size/fill assumptions. Low feature correlation alone does not
prove diversified cash losses. This is a proposed comparison, not an empirical
result about either factor.

## Keep a factor dossier useful to its consumer

One admitted version records definition/orientation/units, economic account or
open role, as-of inputs/coverage, target/policy/maturity, transformations/model,
trial and shortlist history, frozen confirmation/comparator/uncertainty, incremental
use, turnover/liquidity/size conditions, failure cases and current disposition.
Use project records for these facts; this is a content requirement for reuse,
not a mandatory new document for every brief inquiry.

## Monitoring one layer cannot diagnose every failure

Assume a fixed forecast's mature Brier loss is unchanged while spreads and actual
costs double. Inspect participation/capacity and cash before changing the model.
If input availability fails, suspend the affected route first. If properly observed
forecast loss deteriorates, investigate target, conditional scope and baseline with
its uncertainty. Retirement requires a disposition about the version's useful role;
a temporary drawdown neither proves permanent decay nor guarantees recovery.

At twelve looks with per-look false-alarm probability 0.05, the union upper bound
is 0.6. Under an illustrative independence assumption, probability of at least one
false alarm is \(1-0.95^{12}\approx0.45964\). Actual dependent reviews require
their scheduled error policy. Repeatedly inspecting a holdout changes its use.

## Cache and restore with explicit semantics

Freeze input/source versions, availability joins, target/fill policy, operation,
parameters and numerical conventions. A cache containing final revised releases
cannot answer an old as-of request. A partially written result has incomplete
status, even if its score field exists. Replaying a frozen snapshot checks exact
identities where appropriate and declared numerical tolerances elsewhere; a
mismatch requires diagnosis before reuse.

With 20% serial work, an ideal four-worker speedup is
\(1/[0.2+0.8/4]=2.5\), below four; infinite workers are bounded by five.
Measure complete preparation/evaluation/communication/recovery, then remove
duplicate work or parallelize the demonstrated bottleneck. A fast invalid
evaluator remains an invalid experiment.
