# Usual First Experiments

These plans bind the usual paths to small, inspectable starting experiments.
No market data or backtest has been run for these plans. Their constants are
development starting points; user/project constraints and versioned instrument
facts govern real use. General deductions belong to the linked knowledge entries.

## Copper-style daily timing

**Question:** can available state improve attainable directional/policy payment
over simple controls? Use one declared actual futures family and historical
eligible contracts. Keep adjusted signal series distinct from holdings/rolls.
Bind next feasible entry, close-observed exit triggers and later fills.

Start with twenty-return scale, barriers at two scales and ten-session deadline,
as in [policy examples](payouts-barriers.md). Use the basic multiscale panel,
then simultaneous curve/open-interest additions where historical availability
exists. Fit unconditional/regularized probability controls, then the shallow
three-class tree pilot. Compare chronological mature-outcome loss/calibration,
class payments and payoff participation. A frozen momentum-side meta gate is a
separate comparison on forward candidates.

After factor/whole-route confirmation, use a fixed capped inverse-volatility risk
budget and actual-contract cash/variation/roll replay. Estimate size-aware costs;
retain gaps, timeouts and rejected fills. Compare no trade and the same simple
economic policy under the same risk and execution. The result decides whether
state, fit or realization needs repair before capacity expansion.

## A-share relative selection

**Question:** does a same-date relative model improve a feasible benchmark-relative
holding policy? Use historical index eligibility including exits/delistings,
past industry/group definitions, release-aware financial fields and completed-
session state. Bind permitted entry/exit, resale and borrow constraints. A fresh
holding with delayed sale needs a target at its achievable horizon.

Start with own/sector moves, volume anomaly, limit-context features where verified,
and multiscale risk. Rank feasible horizon outcomes within each historical date.
Compare simple continuation/reversed scores, shared linear/context controls and
shallow trees. Add the restrained set pilot only for a supported cross-row gap;
batch/split by date and test mask/permutation consistency.

For an unlevered long-only teaching comparison, declare per-name active bounds
of ±0.5% of equity relative to benchmark, floor total name weights at zero and
cap one-way rebalance turnover \(\tfrac12\sum_i|w_i-w_i^-|\) at 10%. These
illustrative constraints may be infeasible with other requirements; check feasibility
and adjust on development with recorded policy changes. Fix named industry/size
exposures and tune score/risk/trading coefficients in development. Replay actual
inventory, limit/no-fill/delay and net benchmark-relative value. Do not assume
500 names supply 500 independent bets.

## Multi-asset structural allocation

**Question:** can supported risk structure give a feasible allocation without a
mean forecast? Use the [risk-budget experiment](risk-allocation.md): economic
proxy coverage, past-only stabilized covariance, ERC with residual checks and
monthly later fills. Compare equal capital and inverse volatility, stress common
shock/cash constraints, then test a bounded trend overlay separately. A claim of
small drawdowns or superior returns requires the actual net experiment.

## Crypto discontinuities and funding

**Question:** which available state changes an attainable policy after a sharp
move or funding dislocation? Start with one venue/product, historical listings
and continuous-session clocks. Define eligible events independently of the
candidate detector when evaluating coverage. Compare diffusion, ordinary thin-book
liquidity, promotion and forced-liquidation accounts without assigning cause from
one price spike. Only authorized, historically available communications/position
records can discriminate those mechanisms.

Use coarse direction or attainable first-passage probabilities with state for
returns, spread/depth, risk, mark/index basis and published funding. Keep payout
tails, gaps and collateral distinct from the class forecast. Apply a no-trade
payment gate, capped prefunded exposure and actual venue deadlines. For a funding
pair, start from the signed basis/funding identity before a rate forecaster.
Stress reversed funding, widening basis, rejected transfer, collateral haircuts
and custody failure. Confirm versus cash/no trade; a mechanism can remain open
while the scoped predictive comparison is useful.

## Stock/future-linked token inquiry

**Question:** what economic relation and hedge can the wrapper actually support?
First obtain issuer final terms, reference/roll methodology, rights/distributions,
redemption eligibility/fees, settlement, venue trading and reference-market clocks.
Trace a small cash-flow example. A dated future, rolling index and perpetual
tracking the same name can have different payoffs.

Compare executable sides only when the rights and delivery bridge support them.
Otherwise specify uncertain convergence or a reference-session hedge gap. An
unavailable contract/source stops the hard-replication claim at that exact edge;
it still permits a descriptive distinction or data requirement. Test adverse
reference reopening, issuer/custody failure and multi-leg funding separately.
See [instrument context](../knowledge/market-instruments.md) and
[relation fixtures](relations-events.md).

## Pricing and hedge computation

Use the [surrogate pilot](surrogate-hedging.md): bind contract/model/state,
replication/reference fixtures, price/Greek error budgets and fallback; choose
smooth approximation only when its checked repeated-use economics justify it.
For friction-aware hedging, begin with discrete delta and an inventory-aware band
on the same ledger/law/constraints. Independent paths and stress assumptions
determine its bounded result, not mean training price error.
