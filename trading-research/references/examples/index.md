# Reference Examples

Select a worked case after locating its general principle. These resources own
illustrative inputs, settings, calculations and source-scoped practice accounts.
They are independent of the AI research loop and of held-back behavioral tests.
Synthetic calculations demonstrate a method; experiment plans specify work that
has not been run. Neither establishes real-market performance.
