# Market Style and Instrument Context

Use this entry to bind a proposed phenomenon to its market, product rights and
attainable actions. Begin with an instrument/clock/cash map and conditional
behavioral hypotheses. A market nickname or a familiar ticker does not supply
the data, mechanism or executable payoff the inquiry needs.

## Separate exposure from legal and cash identity

For each instrument, establish issuer/venue, underlying reference, currency,
multiplier, ownership or contractual claim, dividends/distributions, expiry/roll,
redemption/conversion, borrow, settlement, marking, collateral and permitted
participants. Bind the applicable terms and historical versions for the study.
Reference-market, product-trading, data-release, funding and redemption clocks may
differ. A stale reference price while a wrapper trades creates reference-session
risk, not a certified discount to an executable share.

Kraken's xStocks disclosure describes economic exposure without underlying-share
ownership/voting rights and identifies issuer/fees and redemption conditions.
Its linear derivatives documentation separately specifies settlement/funding
and margin conventions. These are product-specific examples, not rules for all
stock or futures-linked tokens. Consult issuer final terms and the selected
venue/contract version for any cash-flow equivalence or hedge claim.
[xStocks disclosure](https://www.kraken.com/legal/xstocks),
[linear contract specifications](https://support.mtf.kraken.com/hc/en-us/articles/25499987607837-Linear-Contract-Specifications).

Distinguish a custodial equity-linked claim, a perpetual tracking equity prices,
a dated future, a rolling futures exposure and a leveraged/rebalancing token.
Their basis, distributions, path dependence and financing differ. A futures-linked
token may require a roll index rather than delivery of a particular contract.
Leverage resets can change cumulative return even with the same start/end
reference price. Reconstruct contractual cash flows before applying
[replication/carry](arbitrage-events.md).

## A-share short-horizon behavior is a research family

Potential accounts include attention spreading within a changing group, supply
constraints around price limits, inventory exits by earlier holders, and shocks
shared by an industry/theme. Begin with an as-of group definition, individual
event order and a feasible next action. A high-turnover group may represent
continuing diffusion or fresh demand absorbing earlier inventory exits; one scalar
participation count cannot settle that opposition. Use
[concept construction](market-concepts.md) to choose the distinguishing observation.

For any short-horizon policy, bind resale eligibility, board/listing/status-specific
limits, auctions/halts, borrow availability and price/depth needed for fills. If a
newly bought holding cannot exit until a later permitted session, a same-day stop
is not the attainable label. A limit price print is not evidence that the required
queue quantity could fill. Current general exchange pages locate rule categories;
historical strategy claims require the rule effective in their period and actual
opportunity constraints.
[SSE trading mechanism](https://english.sse.com.cn/start/trading/mechanism/).

**First path:** historical eligible universe → past-defined groups and event
cutoffs → coarse relative/policy outcome at a feasible horizon → small context
panel and restrained ranking/probability control → long/short permissions and
holding constraints → delay/limit/no-fill ledger → net chronological confirmation.
Do not equate number of names with independent breadth.

## Crypto jumps need competing process accounts

Thin-book demand, coordinated promotion, liquidation cascades, information releases
and ordinary volatility can all produce a sharp move followed by reversal. The
CFTC's advisory identifies pump-and-dump schemes in thin/new virtual assets;
it does not classify every spike as manipulation.
[CFTC advisory](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/beware_virtual_currency_pump_dump.html).

Construct an eligible event set independently of the detector if claiming event
coverage. A liquidation account calls for position/funding, mark/index and forced-
order timing; promotion calls for time-located communications and participant
evidence. Price-only forecasting can remain useful with the mechanism open.
Compare probability calibration, tail payouts and feasible exit after gaps.
Venue-specific sampling, wash-like volume uncertainty, fragmented liquidity,
stablecoin/issuer exposure and continuous-session cash deadlines can change the
observation and payout. Verify those facts for the selected study instead of
imposing one universal crypto model.

**First path:** one declared venue/product and past eligibility → independent
event definition → as-of price/liquidity/funding/context state → direction or
attainable policy probabilities → conditional tail/cost participation → capped
prefunded exposure → gap, funding-reversal and inaccessible-cash stress → independent
net confirmation. Add cross-venue or stock/future-linked products after their
rights and joint ledger are established. Absent those terms, retain a descriptive
spread or data requirement and withhold the hard-arbitrage conclusion.
