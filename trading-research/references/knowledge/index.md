# Quantitative Research Knowledge

Select reasoning, prerequisites and a usual first construction by question.
Read prerequisites before the choice they govern; valid prior work can satisfy
them. Brief explanations and fixed rules need only applicable categories.
Changed premises reopen affected work through the research loop.

## Usual complete practice paths

For a new quantitative design, follow the applicable path far enough to connect
target and state to their consumer. The relation selects the path; these are not
extra stages for a fixed-rule test or brief explanation.

**Trend:** executable policy → direction/first-passage probabilities → as-of small
economic panel → restrained proper-loss model → honest factor/route confirmation →
payoff participation → capped volatility risk → actual-contract fill/cash replay.

**Relative selection:** historical feasible set/horizon → same-date relative target
and context → shared/control scores, then a justified set relation → constrained
benchmark-relative holdings → drifted inventory, delay/no-fill costs → net confirmation.

**Structural allocation:** economic coverage → past-only stabilized covariance →
risk budgets/solver residuals → exposure and scenario/funding stress → feasible
rebalances → separately confirmed bounded forecast supplements.

**Factor factory:** economic seed/typed grammar → frozen scorer and attempt budget →
bounded GP control or matched learned proposals → full trial record → frozen shortlist
and independent incremental confirmation → defined aggregation/holding consumer →
three-layer monitoring and versioned replacement.

**Relations/events:** cash-flow rights and executable sides, or a frozen residual
dynamic, or as-of calibrated event/time payments → stateful participation and legs →
prefunding/collateral/residual-fill path → net independent comparison. The type of
relation selects the method; a visible spread alone does not.

**Pricing/hedging:** contract/model/state → finite replication and numerical oracle →
sampling/discretization/value/Greek budgets → checked representation and fallback →
amortized computation or same-ledger friction-policy comparison. Accurate computation
of an admitted pricing map is separate from forecasting market returns.

Retain the original question and supported results when source or realization
limits change the attainable role; reopen only affected dependencies.
