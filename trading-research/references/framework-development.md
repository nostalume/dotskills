# Build and Revise a Trading Framework

Use when learning a decision method from sources or constructing/revising a
working framework. Produce relations that make and update scoped judgments on
new cases. Fixed-rule tests and ordinary case applications retain their owner.

## Learn a useful financial operation

Recover the project's usable method and the capability still missing from its
purpose. Use the [research preferences](knowledge/market-concepts.md#research-preferences)
to compare consequential learning, attainable evidence and cost. Reading another
clear case earns investment when it can improve that capability, rather than
merely repeat a relation already mastered.

From sources, recover premises, observable inputs and the operation connecting
them to a conclusion. Consult the [financial operation index](knowledge/index.md),
then apply the operation to admitted records or an explicitly illustrative case.
Explain the consequence and where it fails. Distinguish attributed teachings,
derived links and proposed adaptations; source fidelity does not prove profit.
Preserve user-fixed definitions. Where possible, fill a missing link with a
justified provisional interpretation, range or sensitivity comparison; do not
invent levels, probabilities or thresholds to make the system appear complete.

## Explore by changing a consequential relation

Let the obstruction suggest a transformation. For example:

- When one observation fits rival processes, derive a condition or comparison
  on which their judgments differ, then seek that evidence.
- When a desired quantity is unidentified or inaccessible, derive an attainable
  bound/comparison and its decision role; keep the original unresolved claim.
- When a result loses time, holdings or cash-path context, restore the state
  needed to test whether the relation survives feasible participation.
- When a relation adds little in its proposed role, test a justified other role
  such as candidate generation, risk or exit; do not call that recovery proof
  of its original role.
- When relations are complementary, compose them under common information and
  participation constraints. When exceptions proliferate, seek a smaller relation
  that explains the differences and retest its consumers.

These are conditional moves, not a direction menu, required sequence or candidate
quota. Candidates should differ in consequences, assumptions or feasible uses,
not just names or thresholds. Choose and perform the next attainable operation
that could change adoption or investment. Keep a promising incomplete account
when its next check warrants the cost; repeat neither uninformative probes nor
already adequate refinements by default.

## Compose and accumulate the working method

Connect opportunity selection and available information to estimates,
price-dependent choices, current holdings/risk, feasible participation and updates
within the chosen scope. Trace an actual case through the relevant relations.
Coordinate rules by role, context and time scale; resolve conflicts or preserve
the exact consequential disagreement. Qualitative judgment need not become a
numerical classifier. A blocking decision gap leaves a partial method; a usable
working system, evidence of net advantage and operational readiness are distinct.

Adopt a relation for a distinction it can make in observations, attainable choices
or risk. Keep it recoverable in existing project/task records for the next user:
the operation and input meaning, applicability, source/assumptions, checked use,
evidence status and consequential failure can supply what that consumer needs.
Prefer a small set of reusable relations to a new rule for each case. This is
project knowledge, not automatic mutation of the installed skill, a compulsory
ledger or a demand to save every thought.

Retrieve and actually use the adopted method on new material. Choose cases that
stress its missing capability, including information and participation conditions.
Declare the observation cutoff; premise changes or removed observations should
change the affected judgment for the stated reason. Preserve earlier versions
when later consumers need to distinguish learning from hindsight. Development
cases do not become independent confirmation through repetition or renaming.

## Let use correct adoption and investment

When evidence challenges the method, locate the changed observation, scope, rule,
priority or core relation; revise the smallest affected dependency and revisit
its consumers. Do not rescue each outcome with an exception, silently narrow the
target, or rewrite a past judgment with later information. A correct explanation
can lose its proposed decision role while retaining another useful one.

For supplied actual decisions/outcomes, use
[realized-decision review](knowledge/lifecycle-computation.md#review-realized-decisions-and-relation-roles).
Its reconciled alternatives and role comparisons feed back into what the framework
adopts and where research effort goes, as well as estimates or execution settings.
Quote snapshots and constructed examples alone are not a realized trading ledger.
No fixed case count, pass rate or per-turn review governs learning.

For `price-action` application, supply definitions, context/priority rules,
observable conditions, revision logic, scope and cutoff. That skill, when
available, owns compatible interpretation and requested conditional plans;
new definitions and framework revisions remain research. If unavailable, complete
supported research and identify the missing capability. Construction adds no
live-advice, acquisition or order authority.
