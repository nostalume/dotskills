# Dialectical inquiry across activities

This companion makes the shared orientation concrete when method selection,
transfer or reflection needs it. It is not a skill router, exhaustive taxonomy,
mandatory intake form or additional workflow. The question may cross several
activities or fit none of these examples. Existing domain knowledge and selected
skills supply the actual definitions, procedures and effects.

## A connected account of knowing and doing

The object and its conditions constrain inquiry. Inquiry selects abstractions
that disclose relevant relations; those abstractions are useful to the extent
that they explain, reconstruct or help transform the concrete subject. What is
selected depends on the question, available knowledge and consequences, so a
representation must carry both its useful distinctions and its limits.

Contradiction analysis traces how a process constitutes opposed yet mutually
dependent tendencies and reproduces or changes their opposition. Determinate
negation identifies which concrete determination becomes untenable and what
content remains. Sublation reconstructs their unity: earlier determinations lose
their independent or universal standing and survive with changed roles in a new
form. Establish that transition through the object's operations and conditions.
Its decisive contradiction can change; two labels or a favored goal do not
establish it. These are connected questions, not a universal derivation scheme.

Action may change the object, its environment and the inquirer's available
knowledge. Feedback then supports a revision of the explanation or construction,
with valid parts retained. Human practice includes social relations, institutions
and consequences for participants; a tool trace or benchmark captures only a
bounded part of that practice. A formal proof, an empirical test, an interpretation
and an ethical argument therefore close different obligations. They can inform
each other while retaining their distinct grounds.

## Transfers into concrete operations

These mappings illustrate how the same orientation changes actual work. Choose
the relevant relation and evidence rather than repeating the vocabulary.

| Activity or question | Construct and operate on the relevant relations | Appropriate support and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural, social or interdisciplinary research | Distinguish phenomenon, construct, mechanism and competing explanations; select observations or interventions that separate them. Let institutions and observer effects enter when they affect the object. | Establish measurement meaning, admissible comparison and inferential reach. Correlation, prediction and causal explanation remain distinct; an open question can remain open. |
| Mathematical, logical or theoretical work | State the objects, domain and assumptions, build a derivation, and use counterexamples or obstructions to locate the condition on which a claim turns. | A valid proof can close the formal claim without execution. A formal model's application to the world requires its own bridge. Dialectical vocabulary cannot repair an inconsistent derivation. |
| Financial, economic or market inquiry | Relate observations to the population, information available at the relevant time, institutions and possible participant responses. Distinguish a useful description, forecast, explanation and proposed action. | Account for selection and changing conditions; evaluate an action claim against attainable timing, costs and execution. A selected historical fit does not establish future advantage, and research authority is separate from trading authority. |
| Software, engineering or other technical development | Derive a construction from intended use, mechanisms, interfaces, resources and lifecycle. Separate stable obligations from contextual means; inspect how changes affect other owners. | Exercise the promised behavior and meaningful failures in the relevant environment. Reuse compatible resources; environment labels or one successful fixture alone do not settle applicability. |
| Design, visual or other creative development | Identify the experience or meaning to produce, material and medium, audience activity and constraints. Develop form through those relations, preserving useful possibilities while refining a coherent whole. | Inspect or experience the actual work where its claim depends on perception or interaction. Formal consistency, fidelity to a brief and user response are different observations; taste is not a discovered natural law. |
| Explanation, writing, interpretation or translation | Reconstruct the source or argument through the reader's prerequisites; develop concepts by their role and carry established meaning into the next step. Consider historical and linguistic context. | Check fidelity, continuity and inferential bridges. A compelling narrative does not establish a factual or causal claim; competing readings may remain when the material does not decide between them. |
| Philosophical, ethical or speculative inquiry | Clarify concepts and starting commitments, expose implications and counterpositions, and examine what a proposed distinction makes intelligible or leaves unexplained. | Separate conceptual coherence, textual interpretation, empirical support and normative justification. Changed premises can yield different conclusions; a value choice is not settled by observational success alone. |
| Practical planning, coordination or environment work | Relate the desired outcome to capabilities, dependencies, authority and people who perform or bear the work. Choose a bounded operation with an observable result. | Verify the actual consumer and surviving effects. A missing capability can limit one operation without making unrelated work impossible; observation and action retain different authority. |

## Worked distinctions and transfers

A compressive wave can evolve from smooth initial data until the gradient becomes
unbounded. If that breakdown follows from the equation's own evolution, more
precision cannot supply the missing global smooth solution. Recover the integral
conservation relation and transfer derivatives to smooth test functions; this
constructs a weak solution concept that admits discontinuities and contains the
classical regime. Admissibility still needs its own basis, such as an entropy
condition. The change concerns what counts as a solution and how it is checked.
A convergent numerical scheme for that solution can remain useful. See the
[conservation-law construction](https://web.stanford.edu/class/math220a/handouts/conservation.pdf).
This illustrates a theoretical reconstruction, not a proof that every physical
change instantiates one philosophical pattern.

A mathematical counterexample can expose a false auxiliary lemma without
refuting the main conjecture, or force a change in the conjecture's objects and
domain. Locate the failed operation before repairing it. A new definition must
support the needed operations and consequences; excluding the counterexample by
wording alone supplies neither. Preserve a narrower theorem and its bridge, while
keeping the original gap visible. A successful new relation can also generate a
construction before any failed attempt. Compare [proof and counterexample](https://classes.matthewjbrown.net/teaching-files/hps/lakatos.pdf).

A market study's attractive fit among many tried variants needs evaluation of
selection as well as of the selected rule. If acting at scale changes liquidity
or costs, treating those conditions as fixed can reproduce the very limitation
the actor encounters. A model of that interaction may preserve the earlier
small-participant case while changing the scale question and its evaluation.
Establish the actual mechanism and new consequence; do not assume that success
must destroy every advantage. A requested fixed-rule empirical test retains its
own task and research authority does not grant trading authority.

A design with local decisions and one shared submission can become fragmented
when each module duplicates the whole final workflow. Reorganize inputs, evidence
and feedback around each local decision while retaining the shared submission.
The former whole-workflow requirement loses its role as a rule for every part;
the final commitment remains a property of their joint organization. Inspect the
actual result and preserve its guarantees. This relation can suggest a research
or coordination construction, whose concrete guarantees need separate grounds.

In interpretation or deliberation, repeated exceptions can reveal that a concept
or criterion excludes something constitutive of its own subject. Reconstruct
that exclusion and what a changed distinction makes intelligible; a new reading
must still account for the source, and a normative choice needs explicit values.
A revised vocabulary alone settles neither. Action can also change the object,
so re-examine affected observations and retain unaffected knowledge.

## Intellectual sources and limits of transfer

These works inform questions and methods; they are neither a unified consensus
nor evidence that an AI implementation will improve. Scientific claims, historical
influence, philosophical interpretation and the engineering transfers above need
their own support. Acknowledging a source does not import all of its judgments.

- Hegel, [Phenomenology, Introduction](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/ph/phintro.htm) and [sublation in the Logic](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/hegel/works/hl/hlbeing.htm): determinate negation, changed criteria and the transformed standing of preserved content; not a ready-made empirical method.
- Marx, [Theses on Feuerbach](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1845/theses/theses.htm): human activity, practice and social relations.
- Engels, [Dialectics of Nature, II](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1883/don/ch02.htm): relations and development investigated through their concrete processes, rather than a thought scheme imposed on them.
- Mao, [On Practice](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-1/mswv1_16.htm) and [On Contradiction](https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-1/mswv1_17.htm): movement between experience, conceptual knowledge and practice; particular conditions and changing principal relations.
- Ilyenkov, [The Abstract and the Concrete](https://www.marxists.org/archive/ilyenkov/works/abstract/abstra1d.htm) and [Contradiction as a Category of Dialectical Logic](https://www.marxists.org/archive/ilyenkov/works/essays/essay10.htm): concrete mediation and concept development beyond adding external factors.
- Levins, [The Strategy of Model Building in Population Biology](https://www.surplusvalue.org.au/Marxism/Levins-1966-Model_Building.pdf), and Lewontin, [The Organism as Subject and Object of Evolution](https://sesultan.web.wesleyan.edu/pdfs/lewontin_organism_as_subject.pdf): question-selected abstraction, complementary models and organism/environment feedback. These do not prescribe a universal tradeoff theorem or fixed model ensemble.
- Engeström, [Expansive Learning at Work](https://doi.org/10.1080/13639080020028747): historically formed activity systems, tools, rules and division of labor; structural tensions have a concrete organizational history.
- Qian Xuesen, [Further Discussion of Open Complex Giant Systems](https://sass.usst.edu.cn/2021/1102/c12541a258347/page.htm): combining qualitative inquiry, human judgment and quantitative models. Its particular scientific assertions require separate evaluation.
- Bailey and colleagues, [The Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253): a domain-specific example of why model selection and an attractive fitted result need distinct evaluation.

- Žižek, [The Parallax View, extract](https://www.lacan.com/zizparallax.htm), and Adorno, [Negative Dialectics, translation](https://libcom.org/book/export/html/397): distinct challenges to external standpoints and automatic reconciliation. Their differences remain questions for interpretation; a perspective change alone establishes no scientific result.

Related developments such as Bhaskar's dialectical critical realism and
contemporary philosophy of technoscience can be studied when their distinctions
serve a real question. Their terminology is not a prerequisite for ordinary work.
